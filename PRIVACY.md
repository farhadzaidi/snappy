# Privacy

Snappy collects nothing about you and sends nothing anywhere. It never uses the network: no accounts, analytics, telemetry, crash reports, ads, or update checks.

## What stays on your computer

Snappy keeps a few files on your own computer, in its own folders:

- **Settings:** your layouts, zones, shortcuts, workspaces, and preferences.
- **State:** which window sits in which zone and workspace, so Snappy can put things back after a restart.
- **Logs:** short records of what Snappy did, such as a drag that didn't snap. They hold window positions and sizes, app identifiers, and timings, never window titles, window contents, or what you type. The detailed debug log is off unless you turn it on.

You can see where these files are in Snappy's settings, and removing them removes everything Snappy knows.

## Permissions

Snappy asks for the permissions it needs to move windows: Accessibility on macOS, and on some systems the right to watch the keyboard while you drag a window or hold Caps Lock as the Snappy key. It uses them only for that: it looks only at the keys it reacts to, such as the drag key and Caps Lock, and never records what you type.

## App stores

If you install Snappy from the Mac App Store or the Microsoft Store, the store's own terms cover the store itself, such as your purchase and any crash reports you agree to share with Apple or Microsoft. Snappy adds nothing to that.
