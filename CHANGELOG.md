# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — unreleased

The first public release.

- Zones and layouts for every display: eight ready-made layouts and an editor for your own.
- Snapping by dragging with a key held, or with shortcuts made of the Snappy key and one other key, which you can choose; Caps Lock can act as the Snappy key.
- Swapping or stacking when a zone is taken, keyboard focus by direction, and undo and redo of window moves.
- Workspaces that hide and bring back groups of windows, and a selection to move or take along several windows at once.
- macOS 14 or later, Windows 11, Linux on X11, and GNOME 46 to 49 and KDE Plasma 6.8 on Wayland.
